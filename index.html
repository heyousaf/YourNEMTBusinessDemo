Our Services

Transportation for every level of care

From routine clinic visits to specialized medical transport, we match the right vehicle and trained driver to every ride.

Ambulatory Rides

For clients who can walk with minimal assistance to and from the vehicle.

  • Doctor & clinic visits
  • Pharmacy runs
  • Physical therapy

Wheelchair Transport

ADA-compliant vehicles with hydraulic lifts and full safety securement.

  • Manual & power chairs
  • Dialysis appointments
  • Hospital discharges

Stretcher Service

For bed-bound patients requiring gurney transport with trained attendants.

  • Facility-to-facility
  • Hospital transfers
  • Long-distance transport

Senior Rides

Patient, courteous drivers who understand the needs of older adults.

  • Adult day programs
  • Family visits
  • Grocery & errands

Dialysis Transport

Reliable recurring rides scheduled around your treatment plan.

  • 3x/week scheduling
  • Same driver when possible
  • Return-trip guarantee

Long Distance

Comfortable, safe transport to specialist appointments statewide.

  • Out-of-town clinics
  • Family reunification
  • Custom routing
